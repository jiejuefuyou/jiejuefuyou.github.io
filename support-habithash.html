HabitHash on the App Store

This page documents version 1.0.5 and later. Feature availability may differ if the installed or store version is older; the starter-pack trial is part of the prepared update.

Free and Pro

Free includes five regular habits, a 30-day history view, daily schedules and one daily reminder across all habits. A starter pack can be tried once. Pro is a one-time purchase for unlimited habits, per-habit reminders, starter packs, selected-day and weekly-goal schedules, and 90-day and yearly insight views. The purchase screen shows your local store price.

Reminders

Enable a reminder while creating or editing a habit. The app requests system notification permission when you enable it. If permission is denied, enable notifications in system settings before expecting reminders. Free users can have one reminder; Pro can configure a reminder for each habit.

Schedules and insights

Mark a habit complete on the relevant day to update its history. Pro schedules can use selected weekdays or a weekly completion goal. Heatmaps and insight summaries are computed on the device. HabitHash does not provide a watch app, widgets or CSV/JSON export.

Purchases

Use Restore Purchases while signed in to the same store account as the original purchase. If products are unavailable, check the connection and store sign-in, then choose Retry. A cancelled or pending purchase does not unlock Pro.

Language and storage

Choose from English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Korean, Spanish, French and German in the app's settings. Habits and completion records are stored locally. The app has no account or cross-device sync. Removing the app can remove these records; system backups depend on your device settings.

Requirements and privacy

Requires iOS 17 or later. Designed for iPhone.

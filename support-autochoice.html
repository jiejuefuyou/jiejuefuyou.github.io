What the app does

Spin a wheel to pick a choice, save decision lists and revisit recent results. Premium adds weighted choices, themes, saved daily wheels and card sharing.

Free and paid upgrade

Free includes two saved lists, ten choices per wheel and 25 history entries. Premium is a one-time purchase for unlimited lists and choices, eight additional themes and a history view of up to 100 entries. The purchase screen displays the current local store price.

Purchases and language

Use Restore Purchases with the store account that made the original purchase. If products cannot load, check the connection and store sign-in, then use Retry. The interface supports English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Korean, Spanish, French and German.

Storage

Choices, saved lists, history and preferences are stored locally. There is no app-provided cloud sync. Cards you choose to export or share go to the destination you select. Removing the app can remove local data; system backups depend on your settings.

FAQ

What is Reverie Reaper?

An auto-shoot bullet-survivor roguelite built for short sessions. Your ship fires automatically — the twist is stop = fire: you drag to move and release to anchor and shoot. A run lasts about three minutes. Pick from eight character hulls, evolve your weapons, and take on four cosmic bosses.

How do I play with one hand?

Everything is a single thumb in portrait. Drag anywhere on screen to steer; let go to stop and open fire. On level-up you draft one of a few upgrade cards. That's the whole control scheme.

Where is my progress saved?

All progress — meta upgrades, unlocks, settings — is stored only on your device. There is no account and no cloud in the current version. Deleting the app permanently removes all saved data.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Reverie Reaper is fully playable offline.

What languages are supported?

English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Korean, Spanish, French, and German. Change it any time in Settings → Language; the whole game switches instantly.

How do I restore my purchases on a new device?

Open Settings inside the app and tap Restore Purchases. You'll need the same Apple ID (or Google account) you bought with.

Is it pay-to-win?

No. Every piece of content is unlockable through normal play. Purchases skip grinding — they don't sell power you can't earn. There's no energy gate, and rewarded ads (revive, free chest, double gold) are always optional.

What age rating is it?

12+. Cartoon style, no blood or gore, no excessive violence.

Troubleshooting

  • Game won't launch / black screen: Force-quit and reopen. If it persists, restart the device and make sure the OS is up to date.
  • Lost progress: Progress lives only on the device. If you deleted and reinstalled the app, local save data is gone by design (no cloud yet). Email us if something looks wrong.
  • A purchase didn't grant: Tap Restore Purchases first. If it still doesn't appear, email us your receipt and we'll sort it out.
  • Refunds are handled by the store: Apple's refund process or Google Play's order history.

Privacy Policy

Reverie Reaper · Effective 14 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the game Reverie Reaper ("the Game", "we", "us") handles information when you play on iOS, Android, or Steam. We built the Game to be a calm, single-player experience and we collect as little as possible.

1. Information We Collect

  • Device & technical data — device model, OS version, language, and coarse region, used to run the Game and fix crashes.
  • Gameplay & analytics — anonymous events (sessions, levels reached, features used) via Apple App Analytics and Mixpanel, used to improve balance and stability. This is not linked to your real-world identity.
  • Advertising identifiers — when you choose to watch a rewarded ad, our mediation partner (AppLovin MAX, which may use AdMob, Meta Audience Network, or Unity Ads) may process an advertising ID to serve and measure ads. On iOS this requires your App Tracking Transparency consent; if you decline, you still get the reward.
  • Purchase records — in-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple or Google. We receive a confirmation that a purchase succeeded; we never receive your payment card or billing details.

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or microphone/camera access.

2. How We Use Information

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the Game and its balance.
  • To diagnose crashes and technical problems.
  • To deliver and measure the rewarded ads you choose to watch.
  • To validate in-app purchases and restore them on your devices.

3. Third-Party Services

The Game uses these services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Apple App Store / Apple App Analytics
  • Google Play / Google Play Billing
  • AppLovin MAX (ad mediation) and its networks: Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads
  • Mixpanel (anonymous product analytics)

4. Advertising

Ads in the Game are opt-in — they only play when you tap a clearly labelled reward (for example, doubling your run rewards). You can remove ads permanently with the "Remove Ads" purchase. You can reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings.

5. Children's Privacy

Reverie Reaper is rated 12+ and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). For users we believe may be children, advertising is configured as non-personalized. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data Retention & Security

Your progress is stored locally on your device (and, on iOS, in your private iCloud key-value store). Analytics events are retained in aggregated form only as long as needed to improve the Game. We use industry-standard measures to protect data, though no method of transmission is 100% secure.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your region (including the EEA/UK under GDPR and California under CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your data, and to object to certain processing. Because we hold almost no identifiable data, most requests can be satisfied by deleting the app and its local save. To exercise any right, contact us below.

8. International Transfers

Our third-party providers may process data in countries other than yours, under appropriate safeguards.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected by a new effective date here. Continued play after an update means you accept the revised policy.

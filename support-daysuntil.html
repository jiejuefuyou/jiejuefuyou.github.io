What the app does

Create countdowns for dates that matter, choose an emoji and color, and optionally enable event reminders or recurring dates.

Free and paid upgrade

Free includes five self-created events, two colors, optional reminders for up to five events and yearly recurrence. Starter events do not use the self-created-event allowance. Pro is a one-time purchase for unlimited events, six additional colors, photo backgrounds, advanced recurrence and optional private iCloud event sync. The purchase screen displays the current local store price.

Purchases and language

Use Restore Purchases with the store account that made the original purchase. If products cannot load, check the connection and store sign-in, then use Retry. The interface supports English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Korean, Spanish, French and German.

Storage

Events and preferences are stored in local app storage. Pro users can enable private iCloud event sync when the required account is available. The developer does not receive the synced event library. Photo backgrounds stay in local storage and do not follow event sync. Removing the app can remove local data; system backups depend on your settings.

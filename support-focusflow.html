Timers and history

Start a focus timer, pause or resume it, and attach a project tag. Completed focus sessions appear in history; breaks are kept out of focus totals. The prepared update adds relaunch recovery: running timers account for elapsed time, while paused timers retain their remaining time. Repeated focus-and-break cycles are available with Pro.

Free and Pro

The free app includes five focus sessions per day, three project tags, standard timers and one focus-and-break cycle. Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited daily sessions and tags, advanced techniques, custom durations and repeated cycles. The purchase screen displays the current local store price.

Purchases and restoration

Open the Pro screen and choose Restore Purchases. Use the same store account as the original purchase. If products cannot load, check your connection and store sign-in, then use Retry. A cancelled or pending purchase does not unlock Pro.

Notifications

Completion reminders use local system notifications. Check the device's notification settings if reminders do not arrive. You can configure a system Focus filter to apply a session length and project tag when you open the app soon after activating that Focus. FocusFlow Lite does not automatically turn your system Focus mode on or off.

Language

The interface supports English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Korean, Spanish, French and German. Choose a language in the app's settings; the default follows the device language.

Storage and troubleshooting

History, tags and timer recovery state are stored locally in the app. The app does not provide account sign-in or cross-device history sync. Removing the app can remove this data; device backup and restoration depend on your system settings.

If a timer or history entry looks wrong, reopen the app and check the app version. Contact support if it persists. For purchase refunds, use Apple's refund process.

Requirements and privacy

Requires iOS 17 or later. Designed for iPhone.

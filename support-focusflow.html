FAQ

What does FocusFlow do?

FocusFlow is a focus timer designed for deep work — like Pomodoro, but with project tags so you can see exactly where your time went. Pick a project (or create a new one), tap start, and FocusFlow tracks the session. Weekly analytics show your top projects by time spent. Optional iOS 17 Focus Filter integration silences distractions during sessions.

What's a project tag?

A label you attach to a focus session. Examples: book-chapter-3, refactor-auth, client-acme. You can have unlimited tags. The weekly analytics rank tags by total minutes — useful for tracking which projects are actually getting your attention.

How does the iOS 17 Focus Filter work?

FocusFlow registers as a Focus Filter in your iPhone Settings. When you start a focus session, it can automatically activate a Focus Mode (e.g., "Work") to silence notifications. When the session ends, the Focus Mode is deactivated. Set it up in Settings → Focus → Work → Add Filter → FocusFlow.

Where is my session data stored?

All sessions, project tags, and analytics are stored only on your device. No account is required. No data is uploaded. If you enable Apple Sign-In + iCloud sync (Premium feature), session data syncs across your devices via iCloud — never our servers.

How do I restore my Premium purchase on a new device?

Open the app → Settings → tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.

The Premium upgrade button shows nothing or fails — what should I do?

  • Confirm your device has a working Internet connection.
  • Settings → App Store → confirm you are signed in to an Apple ID.
  • Force-quit and reopen the app.
  • If still failing, email us with your iOS version and device model.

Weekly analytics show wrong totals — bug?

Pull-to-refresh on the analytics tab usually fixes display lag. If totals stay wrong after a force-quit and reopen, email us a screenshot so we can investigate.

How do I change the language?

Settings → Language → pick from English, 日本語, 简体中文, 繁體中文, 한국어, Español, Français, Deutsch. The change takes effect immediately. Default follows your iPhone system language.

Troubleshooting

  • Focus Filter doesn't auto-activate: Settings → Focus → check FocusFlow is added as a filter to your chosen Focus Mode.
  • App crashes on launch: reboot the device, then update to the latest iOS version. If it persists, email us.
  • Premium not unlocking after purchase: tap Restore Purchases in Settings.
  • Sign in with Apple fails: Settings → Apple ID → Sign-In & Security → confirm Sign in with Apple is enabled. Force-quit and reopen FocusFlow.

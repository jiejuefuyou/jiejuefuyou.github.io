FAQ

What does Thankslate do?

Thankslate shows your tip jar QR codes on demand. Save your payment handles (PayPal, Venmo, WeChat Pay, Alipay, PayPay, LINE Pay, Cash App, Zelle, Revolut, Wise — 10 providers supported), and one tap opens the QR code at full screen. Hand the phone to a customer, friend, or tipper to scan.

How is this different from showing a screenshot of my QR?

Keep your saved payment methods in one place, switch between them, generate QR codes locally from payment links, and use card themes or poster layouts. Imported receive-code images are copied into the app; importing does not remove the original from Photos.

Which payment providers are supported?

  • URL-based QR (you enter a handle or payment URL): PayPal, Venmo, Cash App, Revolut, Wise
  • Imported receive-code image: 微信支付 (WeChat Pay), 支付宝 (Alipay), PayPay (JP), LINE Pay, Zelle

For WeChat / Alipay / PayPay / LINE Pay / Zelle, import your receive-code image from the provider's app (export QR → save to Photos → import in Thankslate). Thankslate displays your code; availability and payment processing are controlled by the provider.

Where are my payment handles stored?

All payment methods are stored only on your device. No account is required. No data is uploaded to any server. Payment methods and imported QR images are saved as local snapshots and separate image files in the app’s Application Support directory. Preferences and the one-time output trial use UserDefaults. The app does not provide cloud sync; device backups depend on your iOS settings.

Does the QR code expire?

Thankslate generates URL-based QR codes locally from your saved payment target. Imported receive codes and payment links can expire or change under the payment provider’s rules. Test your code with the provider and import a fresh code when needed.

Why is there a 1-method limit on the free tier?

The free tier supports one payment method, watermarked card/poster output, and a one-time trial of premium output. Thankslate Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited methods, all card themes, printable poster sizes, and watermark-free output. The purchase screen shows the current price for your App Store region.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new device?

Open the app → tap Settings → Restore Purchase, using the Apple ID that bought Thankslate Pro. The rename preserves the existing purchase, so you do not need to buy it again. Saved payment methods are separate from purchase restoration.

Troubleshooting

  • QR won't scan: increase your screen brightness; some scanners struggle with dim displays. Also try turning on True Tone in Settings.
  • WeChat / Alipay QR not accepted: check that the receive code is still valid in the provider’s app. Export a fresh code and re-import it in Thankslate.
  • PayPal QR link goes to wrong account: open your PayPal method’s edit form and check that the handle or link matches your PayPal.me account.
  • App crashes on launch: reboot the device, then update to the latest iOS.

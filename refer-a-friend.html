FAQ

Why such a generous referral?

Because cold outreach has 1-2% conversion. A warm intro from a friend has 30-50%. The economics work even at $50 each side — for me. And it's the right thing for your friend if they actually need this work.

What if my friend isn't a good fit?

I'll tell them. I disqualify ~90% of inbound — most teams genuinely don't need outside help. You don't get penalized for "wasting" a referral; just don't push it on people who don't need it.

Is there a referral cap?

No cap on number of friends, no cap on cumulative payout. If you refer 10 friends who all become clients, you get $500. If 100, $5,000. Just keep them genuine.

Can I refer myself or my own company?

No self-refer. But if you're a current client and your boss / cousin / co-founder hires me separately, that counts.

What if my friend wants the bundle ($25) instead of consulting?

The $50/$50 program is for consulting only. For the $25 prompt bundle, share your referral link and Gumroad's built-in 10% kickback applies (so $2.50 to you per sale).

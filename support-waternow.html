FAQ

What does WaterNow do?

WaterNow is a no-nonsense hydration tracker. Tap a button to log a glass / bottle / custom amount. See a daily progress ring. Get a friendly reminder every few hours (optional). That's it. No friend challenges. No streaks that punish you for missing a day. No subscription.

What's the daily target?

You set it. Default is 2000 mL (2 liters) — the widely cited general adult target — but real hydration needs vary by body size, activity, climate, and diet. The app lets you set anything from 500 mL to 6000 mL. WaterNow is not medical advice; consult a clinician if you have specific hydration needs.

Does WaterNow read or write to Apple Health?

Not in v1.0. We're considering adding optional HealthKit integration (read existing water entries, write WaterNow entries) in v1.1 — it'll be opt-in, and Apple's HealthKit privacy controls always apply. v1.0 has zero HealthKit access.

Where is my hydration data stored?

All entries are stored only on your device via iOS encrypted standard storage. No account, no cloud, no server. Deleting the app permanently removes all data.

How do I customize the reminder frequency?

Settings → Reminders → choose Off / Every 2 hours / Every 3 hours / Every 4 hours. You can also choose a Quiet Hours window (e.g., no reminders 22:00–07:00). Reminders use iOS local notifications — they fire even when the app is closed but never leave your device.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new device?

Open the app → Settings → tap Restore Purchases. Same Apple ID required.

The progress ring shows wrong total — bug?

Pull-to-refresh on the home screen usually fixes display lag. If the total stays wrong after a force-quit, email us a screenshot — there was a rounding bug in early TestFlight builds (fixed v1.0.1+).

Refund policy

App Store purchases are managed by Apple. To request a refund: Apple's standard refund process.

Troubleshooting

  • Notifications don't fire: Settings → Notifications → WaterNow → confirm Allow Notifications is on, and Lock Screen / Notification Center is checked.
  • Progress ring stuck: pull-to-refresh; if persists, force-quit and reopen.
  • App crashes on launch: reboot, update iOS, contact us if it continues.
  • Premium not unlocking: Settings → Restore Purchases.

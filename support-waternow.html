What WaterNow does

Choose a beverage and tap a preset amount to add it to today's hydration log. WaterNow shows progress toward your daily goal, a 7-day history, achievements, and a plant garden.

WaterNow Pro is a one-time purchase that adds 30-day history, up to eight custom daily reminder times, custom intake amounts, optional effective-hydration weighting, and eight premium plant skins.

Reminders

How do I enable the free reminder?

During onboarding or in Settings → Reminders, turn on the midday reminder. WaterNow asks for notification permission only after that explicit action.

How do custom reminders work?

WaterNow Pro can save up to eight custom daily times. They are local iOS notifications; WaterNow does not operate a push server. Each time is shown in your selected app language and follows the device's local clock after travel or daylight-saving changes.

Notifications are not appearing

  1. Open iOS Settings → Notifications → WaterNow.
  2. Turn on Allow Notifications.
  3. Return to WaterNow → Settings. The app will reconcile the saved reminder schedule.

Local data and recovery

Hydration history is stored in WaterNow's private iOS container. WaterNow 1.0.7 verifies each save and keeps a previous-good local backup. If the main state file cannot be opened, the app attempts recovery and shows a visible message instead of silently replacing the library with an empty history.

WaterNow does not provide cross-device sync. Apple device backups may include app data according to your Apple backup settings.

WaterNow Pro and Restore Purchase

Open Settings → Restore Purchase while signed into the Apple ID that bought WaterNow Pro. A successful restore unlocks Pro. If no prior purchase exists, WaterNow shows that result explicitly rather than reporting a generic failure.

Purchases and refunds are processed by Apple. For refunds, use Apple's refund process.

Totals, units, and goals

  • Stored intake uses milliliters internally; switching to fluid ounces changes display only.
  • Effective hydration is an optional Pro display calculation and is off by default.
  • WaterNow is a logging tool, not medical advice. Follow a clinician's guidance for fluid restriction or medical hydration needs.

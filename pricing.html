❓ FAQ

Why is the Starter so cheap ($14)?

My first 10 clients go at half-price (was $28). The goal is to build real case studies, not to profit on the starter tier. $14 covers my cost for 60 minutes + some prompt design work. What you're buying is "14 days of me shipping 4 iOS apps + 5-side toolkit + 1 WeChat mini-program" compressed into 60 minutes.

How do I choose between $500 vs $1.2k retainer?

5-15 person team + 1-2 workflows → $500/mo is enough.
15-30 person team + 2-3 workflows + cross-department integration → $800-1.2k/mo makes sense.
Not sure? Start with the half-price first month ($250-600) and decide based on actual workload.

Our team has never used AI tools. Can we actually get started?

90% of my clients start from zero. In week 1, I get your team's first workflow fully running — employees only need to know "copy + paste + click." Comfortable in 3-5 days.

How does data security work?

By default, none of my solutions upload sensitive data to GPT/Claude. For workflows involving customer privacy or financial data, we use local models (Qwen / DeepSeek private deployment). All client data is deleted after 30 days (see service-agreement-en.md Section 6).

What if AI makes mistakes?

All AI outputs must be reviewed by an employee before execution — this is a design principle. Not replacing humans, but letting 1 person do the work of 4.

What if I change my mind mid-contract?

Starter: 7-day full refund. Standard retainer: cancel anytime, month-to-month, no cancellation fee. Enterprise: first 7 days full refund, then prorated refund for delivered work.

Can overseas companies use this?

Yes. International clients pay in USD (Starter $14 / Retainer $500-1.2k/mo / Enterprise $2.5k+/mo). Contracts use English version, SIAC arbitration. Contact jiejuefuyou@gmail.com.

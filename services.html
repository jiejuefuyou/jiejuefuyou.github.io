Q: Our company never used AI. Can we actually get started?

90% of my clients start from zero. In week 1, I get your team's first workflow fully running — employees only need to know "copy + paste + click." Comfortable in 3-5 days.

By default, none of my solutions upload sensitive data to GPT/Claude. For workflows involving customer privacy or financial data, we use local models (Qwen / DeepSeek private deployment).

FAQ

What does TipJar Now do?

TipJar Now shows your tip jar QR codes on demand. Save your payment handles (PayPal, Venmo, WeChat Pay, Alipay, PayPay, LINE Pay, Cash App, Zelle, Revolut, Wise — 10 providers supported), and one tap opens the QR code at full screen. Hand the phone to a customer, friend, or tipper to scan.

How is this different from showing a screenshot of my QR?

Three things: (1) One-tap switching between providers if your guest doesn't use the first one. (2) Live QR regeneration for providers that accept dynamic links (PayPal.me, Venmo, Cash App). (3) No photo library bloat — your QR codes aren't sitting in iCloud Photos.

Which payment providers are supported?

  • Auto-generated QR (you enter handle): PayPal, Venmo, Cash App
  • Custom URL or manual QR image: 微信支付 (WeChat Pay), 支付宝 (Alipay), PayPay (JP), LINE Pay, Zelle, Revolut, Wise

For WeChat / Alipay / PayPay / LINE Pay, the provider doesn't expose a public link format, so you upload your own QR image from the provider's app (export QR → save to Photos → import in TipJar Now).

Where are my payment handles stored?

All payment methods are stored only on your device. No account is required. No data is uploaded to any server. Custom QR images you upload are stored in the iOS Documents directory of TipJar Now and are deleted when you delete the app.

Does the QR code expire?

No. The QR code is regenerated each time you open the app from your stored handle or URL — it's never cached on a server, never has a TTL.

Why is there a 1-method limit on the free tier?

To respect Apple's revenue ecosystem (no subscription tax), Pro is a single ¥300 ($1.99) one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited methods. The free tier lets you test the app with one method first — if it works for you, the upgrade is the price of one coffee, forever.

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new device?

Open the app → tap the Pro icon top-left → tap Restore Purchases. Same Apple ID required.

Troubleshooting

  • QR won't scan: increase your screen brightness; some scanners struggle with dim displays. Also try turning on True Tone in Settings.
  • WeChat / Alipay QR not accepted: these providers sometimes invalidate exported QR codes after 7 days. Re-export from WeChat / Alipay and re-import in TipJar Now.
  • PayPal QR link goes to wrong account: check Settings → your PayPal entry → make sure the handle matches your PayPal.me URL.
  • App crashes on launch: reboot the device, then update to the latest iOS.

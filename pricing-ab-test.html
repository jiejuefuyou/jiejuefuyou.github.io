FAQ

Why no monthly auto-billing?

Trust matters more than friction. You pay manually each month so you stay in control. Cancel by sending one Slack DM — no portal, no retention call.

Do you take equity instead of cash?

For Quarterly + Enterprise tiers, yes — up to 50% of the retainer in stock options at the latest valuation. Email me with cap table summary.

How is this different from Cursor / Copilot?

Cursor / Copilot are tools. This is the playbook for using them at your company — which workflows to migrate first, how to measure ROI, what NOT to automate. I've shipped 4 iOS apps + 11 repos in 14 days using these tools; I share the system, not the tool.

What's the 30-day money-back guarantee?

For Starter + Single-month tiers: if you don't see at least 1 actionable win you'll implement, full refund. For Quarterly: prorated to the unused months.

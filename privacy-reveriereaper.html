Reverie Reaper — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-07-03 · Developer: Hao Sun (Tokyo, Japan)

Reverie Reaper is a single-player game. We designed it to need as little of your data as possible. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with information, both today and as new features roll out.

What the current version collects

Nothing personal. The version available today stores your game progress — meta upgrades, unlocks, and settings — only on your device. There is no account, no login, no cloud sync, and no server. The game does not transmit your data anywhere, and it does not require an internet connection to play.

What we never collect

  • Your name, email, phone number, or address
  • Your location
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or health data

The app requests none of these permissions.

Features planned for later versions

As Reverie Reaper grows, some optional services may be added. When they are, this policy will be updated and the behavior will be exactly as described here:

  • Anonymous gameplay analytics. To understand which stages are too hard or too easy, we may record anonymous, aggregated gameplay events (for example, "a run ended on stage 3"). These contain no information that identifies you personally.
  • Optional rewarded ads. We may integrate a third-party advertising provider (AppLovin MAX) to offer optional rewarded ads — for example, watch an ad to revive or double a reward. Advertising SDKs can use a device advertising identifier (such as Apple's IDFA). On Apple devices this is only ever used after you explicitly allow it through the system App Tracking Transparency prompt, and you can change your choice any time in your device settings. Ads are always optional and never required to progress.
  • In-app purchases. Purchases are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see or store your payment details.

Children

Reverie Reaper is rated 12+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children (in line with COPPA and equivalent regulations).

Third-party services

When the features above are enabled, the following providers may process limited data under their own privacy policies:

  • Apple (App Store, in-app purchases)
  • Google (Google Play, in-app purchases)
  • AppLovin (optional rewarded ads)

Deleting your data

Because your data lives on your device, deleting the app permanently removes all of it. There is nothing stored on our side to delete.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Privacy Policy

**FocusFlow** — last updated 2026-04-29

What we collect

**Nothing.** FocusFlow does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The app does not require an account, makes no network requests, includes no analytics SDKs, and includes no third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.

What's stored on your device

The prompts you create — title, body, tags, and per-prompt usage counts — are stored locally in the app's sandboxed Documents directory. This data never leaves your device. Deleting the app removes all stored data.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple via StoreKit. FocusFlow receives only the entitlement status (purchased / not purchased). Apple's own privacy policy governs purchase information: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>.

Children

FocusFlow is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not collect any data, so no data about children is ever processed.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear at this URL with the revised date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at <https://github.com/jiejuefuyou/autoapp-focusflow/issues>.

FocusFlow Lite — Privacy Policy

Updated 2026-10-01.

Developer data collection

FocusFlow Lite has no developer account, advertising or analytics SDK. The developer does not receive your focus history or project tags through the app.

Local storage

The app stores completed focus sessions, project tags, preferences, configured Focus filter markers and active timer recovery state in its local app storage. There is no app-provided cloud history sync. Removing the app can remove this data. Device backup and restoration are governed by your system settings and the backup provider.

Notifications

Optional completion reminders use local system notifications. Notification permission and delivery are managed by the operating system.

Purchases

StoreKit provides product information, purchases, restoration and entitlement checks. These services may communicate with Apple and require a network connection. The app uses verified purchase entitlement information to unlock Pro and does not receive your payment card details. Apple's privacy policy applies to its services.

Links and support

Links you choose to open are handled by the destination website or store. If you email support, the developer receives the information you send; please avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

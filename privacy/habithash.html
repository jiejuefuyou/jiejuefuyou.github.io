Privacy Policy for HabitHash

**Last updated**: 2026-05-17

**Developer**: Hao Sun (jiejuefuyou@gmail.com)

**Effective for**: HabitHash iOS app, v1.0+

What we collect

**Nothing.** HabitHash is a 100% offline habit tracker. We collect zero information about you, your habits, your completion history, your device, or your usage patterns.

Local storage only

Your habits and daily completion history (which days you checked off which habit, custom emoji, color tags, notes) are stored **only on your device**, using iOS's encrypted standard `UserDefaults` JSON encoding.

  • We **never** transmit this data anywhere.
  • We **never** sync it across devices (no cloud, no server, no account).
  • If you delete the app, all data is permanently gone (no recovery possible — your privacy is the trade-off).

Third-party services

None. HabitHash does not include any third-party SDKs (no Firebase, no Crashlytics, no analytics, no ads, no Adjust, no AppsFlyer, no Sentry, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude — nothing).

The only network call is when you tap **Restore Purchases** or **Buy Pro** — this contacts Apple's StoreKit servers per Apple's iOS payment processing. Apple's privacy policy applies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Habit completion tracking

All habit completion ticks are written to local storage via UserDefaults the moment you tap "done" for a habit. There is no telemetry pipeline, no event queue, no anonymized aggregation. The streak counts, monthly summary, and contribution heatmap visualisations you see in the app are computed entirely on-device from the locally-stored data.

Push notifications

None. We do not send remote push notifications. If you opt into local habit reminders in Settings, those notifications are scheduled by iOS itself (via the local UNUserNotificationCenter API) and never leave your device.

App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

We do not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. Per Apple's ATT requirement, this app does not invoke the ATT permission prompt because we have nothing to track.

Children

This app does not target users under 13. It is rated 4+ in App Store Connect because it contains no objectionable content.

Your rights

Since we collect nothing, there is no data to access, correct, or delete. If you want to remove all your saved habits, simply delete the app or use the "Reset all habits" action inside Settings.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be noted in app release notes.
